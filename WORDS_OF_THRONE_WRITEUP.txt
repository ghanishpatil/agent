Step 5: Combining the Parts

According to the challenge instructions, we need to combine the three decoded parts with underscores between them:

Step 6: The Game of Thrones Reference

The challenge title "Words of the Throne" and the description mentioning "Seven Kingdoms" and "Westeros" are references to Game of Thrones. The famous quote "Chaos is a ladder" is spoken by Littlefinger (Petyr Baelish) in the series, representing his philosophy about using disorder and confusion to climb the social and political hierarchy.

The flag cleverly encodes this quote using leetspeak (Ch0a5 = Chaos) combined with the straightforward "IS ladder" to recreate the iconic line.
