### **1. SOC 2 Type II Certification**
**What it is:** Gold standard for security, availability, and confidentiality  
**Cost:** $15,000 - $50,000  
**Duration:** 3-6 months  
**Validity:** 1 year  

**Providers:**
- **Deloitte** - https://www2.deloitte.com/us/en/pages/risk/solutions/soc-examinations.html
- **PwC** - https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/consulting/cybersecurity/soc-reporting.html
- **KPMG** - https://home.kpmg/us/en/home/services/advisory/risk-consulting/internal-audit-risk/soc-examinations.html
- **EY** - https://www.ey.com/en_us/assurance/soc-reports

**Benefits:**
✅ Highest credibility with enterprise clients  
✅ Required for many B2B contracts  
✅ Comprehensive security validation  

### **2. ISO 27001 Certification**
**What it is:** International standard for information security management  
**Cost:** $10,000 - $30,000  
**Duration:** 3-6 months  
**Validity:** 3 years  

**Providers:**
- **BSI Group** - https://www.bsigroup.com/en-US/iso-27001-information-security/
- **SGS** - https://www.sgs.com/en/digital-trust/cybersecurity/iso-27001
- **TUV SUD** - https://www.tuvsud.com/en-us/services/management-systems-certification/iso-27001
- **DNV** - https://www.dnv.com/services/iso-27001-certification-information-security-management-1979

**Benefits:**
✅ Global recognition  
✅ Demonstrates systematic security approach  
✅ Competitive advantage
